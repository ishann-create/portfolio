I grew up on the internet cuz i had extremely early access to it, but my story starts somewhere quieter.

I come from a traditional Indian family, where belief and ritual were part of everyday life. When I was a kid, my parents had my future read by a well-known astrologer. His prediction was heavy: he said I’d grow up to be the most successful and influential person my bloodline has ever seen. To this day, that sits at the back of my head. I wake up everyday trying to be shooting for the stars, so if i fall, i'll land on a cloud.

I choose studying electronics at college because I watched Tron when i was 12yo, which had a huge influence on me and put me in pursuit of tech. I love building stuff. But apart from tech, I’ve spent a major part of my life doing other creative work: media production, leading marketing campaigns, and hosting communities.

I've spent a fair amount of time in social media. I used to work with bands, hospitality, and influencers. Still, no matter how far I wander, I keep coming back to what I love most: building with technology.
